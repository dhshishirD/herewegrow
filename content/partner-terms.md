## Partner Terms (short) / পার্টনার শর্ত (সংক্ষেপে)

**English**
1. You may use HereWeGrow templates and the Builder to make CVs for your clients. You may not resell or share the template files themselves.
2. Do not write false experience, fake certificates or false information in any client CV. Do not sell jobs or visas through this pass. Breaking this rule ends your pass without refund.
3. Client details are private. Keep them safe. You can delete a client folder or ask us for an export at any time. Do not upload NID or passport numbers.
4. One account, up to 2 devices. Fair-use limits apply (client places per period).
5. Payment is once, no auto-renewal. We do not promise any income. What you charge clients is your own decision.
