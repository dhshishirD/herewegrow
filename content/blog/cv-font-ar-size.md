## What is the best font and size for a CV?

Use a clean, common font at 10–12 pt for body text, with headings about 12–16 pt and your name a little larger. Safe choices include Calibri, Arial, Georgia, Times New Roman and similar plain fonts. Avoid decorative or script fonts. Employers and online portals read plain fonts more reliably.

*By Delowar Hassan Shishir, founder of Career Solutions and builder of HereWeGrow. · Last updated: December 2026*

## Recommended sizes

| Part of the CV | Typical size |
|---|---|
| Your name | 18–24 pt |
| Section headings | 12–16 pt |
| Body text and bullet points | 10–12 pt |
| Contact line | 10–11 pt |

Many Bangladeshi university sample CVs use about 10–11 pt for body text and 12 pt for headings. Below 10 pt starts to feel hard to read, so cut content before you shrink the font.

## Safe font choices

- **Sans-serif (modern, clean):** Calibri, Arial, Open Sans, Lato, Roboto, Source Sans
- **Serif (formal):** Georgia, Times New Roman, Garamond, Merriweather

Pick one font family for the whole CV. A second font is fine only for headings. More than two looks messy.

## Fonts to avoid

- Script or handwriting fonts
- Decorative or display fonts
- Very thin or very condensed fonts that blur in print or on a small phone
- A font that your reader's computer may not have: it can be swapped for another and break your layout. A PDF fixes this.

## FAQ

**Is Times New Roman still okay?**

Yes. It is formal and common, and plenty of applicants use it. It is simply less modern than a sans-serif font.

**Can I use a Bangla font on an English CV?**

Only for Bangla text. Keep English sections in an English font so spacing and letters look consistent.

**Does font size affect ATS?**

Size itself rarely matters. Plain fonts and a simple layout do, because portals must read the text. See [CV writing rules](/blog/cv-lekhar-niyom).
